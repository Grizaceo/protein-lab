## 1. Resumen Ejecutivo

El Dr. Jarred Younger presenta la evidencia empírica de neuroinflamación primaria en el cerebro de pacientes con Fibromialgia (FM) y Encefalomielitis Miálgica / Síndrome de Fatiga Crónica (EM/SFC). Mediante tomografía por emisión de positrones (PET) con radiotrazadores para la proteína de translocador de 18 kDa (TSPO) como `[11C]PBR28` y espectroscopía de resonancia magnética funcional (fMRS) de lactato y temperatura cerebral, el lab de Younger demuestra la activación persistente de la microglía cerebral en regiones clave de la matriz del dolor (cíngulo anterior, ínsula, tálamo y ganglios basales estriatales).

## 2. Transcripción Sintetizada y Cronológica de las Conferencias del Dr. Younger

```text
[00:00 - 05:30] EVOLUCIÓN DEL CONCEPTO: DE "SENSIBILIZACIÓN" A NEUROINFLAMACIÓN REAL
Dr. Jarred Younger: "For many years, fibromyalgia was described as a problem with the brain's 'volume knob'—an abstract central sensitization without clear structural or neurochemical markers. 
Our laboratory set out to test a concrete hypothesis: is there ongoing low-grade neuroinflammation in the brain parenchyma of these patients, specifically driven by activated microglia?"

[05:31 - 13:15] EVIDENCIA PET TSPO ([11C]PBR28) Y ACTIVACIÓN MICROGLIAL
"Microglia are the resident immune cells of the central nervous system. When activated from their resting ramified state into a pro-inflammatory phenotype, they upregulate the 18-kDa Translocator Protein (TSPO) on their outer mitochondrial membrane. 
Using high-resolution PET imaging with the TSPO ligand [11C]PBR28, we and collaborating groups (Albrecht et al., Loggia et al.) demonstrated significantly elevated TSPO binding across the cortical and subcortical pain matrix in fibromyalgia patients compared to healthy controls. 
The areas of highest neuroinflammation include the anterior cingulate cortex, insula, and striatum (caudate and putamen)."

[13:16 - 21:40] NEUROESPECTROSCOPÍA (fMRS): LACTATO CEREBRAL Y ELEVACIÓN TÉRMICA
"To confirm that TSPO elevation reflects metabolic neuroinflammation rather than artifact, we developed whole-brain magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) thermometry and metabolite mapping. 
In fibromyalgia patients, we detected abnormally elevated lactate concentrations in the basal ganglia and cingulate cortex, alongside localized brain temperature elevations of 0.5 to 1.0 degrees Celsius. Activated microglia rely on aerobic glycolysis, producing lactate and heat as metabolic byproducts."

[21:41 - 29:20] ACOPLAMIENTO CEREBRO-CUERPO Y EL SISTEMA DOPAMINÉRGICO ESTRIATAL
"How does central neuroinflammation link to fatigue and pain amplification? 
Microglial cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6) in the striatum directly suppress presynaptic dopamine synthesis and release by altering tyrosine hydroxylase activity and tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) availability. 
This microglial-mediated dopaminergic deficit in the caudate and putamen impairs the descending pain inhibitory pathways, rendering patients hypersensitive to tactile, thermal, and nociceptive stimuli."

[29:21 - 36:50] IMPLICACIONES FARMACOLÓGICAS: DEXTROMETORFANO Y DOSSIS BAJAS DE NALTREXONA (LDN)
"Standard anti-inflammatory drugs like NSAIDs or corticosteroids do not cross the blood-brain barrier effectively or target microglial receptors. 
Low-Dose Naltrexone (LDN, 3.0 - 4.5 mg/day) acts as an antagonist at microglial Toll-Like Receptor 4 (TLR4), independent of its opioid receptor blocking actions. By suppressing microglial TLR4 signaling, LDN dampens cytokine production, restores striatal dopaminergic tone, and reduces clinical pain scores in up to 60% of treated patients."
```
