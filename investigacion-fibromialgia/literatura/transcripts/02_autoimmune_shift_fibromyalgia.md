## 1. Resumen Ejecutivo

Este documento aborda la transición paradigmática en la comprensión de la Fibromialgia (FM), pasando del dogma clásico de "sensibilización central pura" a un **modelo integrativo neuroinmune periférico-central**. Se examina la presencia de autoanticuerpos IgG circulantes, el fenómeno de neuropatía de fibra pequeña (SFN) presente en hasta el 50% de los pacientes con FM y los mecanismos por los cuales las inmunoglobulinas provocan hipersensibilidad en los nociceptores aferentes primarios A-delta y C.

## 2. Transcripción Sintetizada y Cronológica del Simposio

```text
[00:00 - 05:00] EL CAMBIO DE PARADIGMA EN DOLOR CRÓNICO
"For over thirty years, fibromyalgia has been defined as a functional somatic syndrome characterized by central amplification of pain signals. However, high-resolution corneal confocal microscopy and skin punch biopsies have revealed epidermal nerve fiber density (IENFD) loss in 40-50% of FM patients. This empirical finding sparked a paradigm shift: peripheral nerve pathology and immune activation are not mere secondary consequences, but primary drivers of the disease."

[05:01 - 12:30] AUTOANTICUERPOS SÉRICOS Y NORMAS DE REACTIVIDAD
"Proteomic screens of autoantibodies in chronic nociplastic pain cohorts have identified IgG targeting structural and functional proteins of peripheral sensory neurons and surrounding glia. 
Unlike classical autoimmune disorders that cause rapid tissue destruction (such as Neuromyelitis Optica or Myasthenia Gravis), FM autoantibodies appear to act as functional modulators. They bind to cell-surface channels and structural gap junction proteins, shifting the resting membrane potential of nociceptors closer to the firing threshold."

[12:31 - 20:15] SENSIBILIZACIÓN DE NOCICEPTORES Y BUCLE DE RETROALIMENTACIÓN
"When pathogenic IgG binds to dorsal root ganglia receptors, it initiates a bi-phasic response:
1. Immediate phase: Direct modulation of voltage-gated sodium channels (Nav1.7 / Nav1.8) and inward rectifying potassium channels, causing spontaneous ectopic discharges.
2. Delayed phase: Recruitment of tissue-resident macrophages and mast cells via Fc-receptor crosslinking, releasing histamine, tryptase, and nerve growth factor (NGF).
This peripheral barrage of ectopic impulses continuously bombards the spinal cord dorsal horn, ultimately driving and maintaining central sensitization."

[20:16 - 27:45] OVERLAP ENTRE FIBROMIALGIA, LONG-COVID Y SÍNDROMES AUTOINMUNES
"Epidemiological and serological analyses demonstrate a striking overlap between post-viral syndromes (e.g., Long COVID / ME-CFS) and Fibromyalgia. In both cohorts, post-infectious loss of B-cell tolerance leads to persistent systemic autoantibody production. The auto-antibodies target autonomic nerve fibers, SGCs, and vascular endothelial cells, explaining both the widespread pain and dysautonomia frequently reported by FM patients."

[27:46 - 35:00] CONSECUSIONES Y MARCADORES BIOLÓGICOS DE SUBTIPO
"To advance targeted treatments, clinical practice must adopt biomarker-driven stratification:
- Subtype A (Peripheral Autoimmune / SFN-positive): High anti-SGC IgG titers, epidermal nerve fiber loss, responsive to IVIG/plasma exchange.
- Subtype B (Central Neurobiochemical / Dysdopaminergic): Intact peripheral fibers, predominant sQTL splice alterations in DRD2/COMT, responsive to dopaminergic agonists (Pramipexole) and central neuromodulation."
```
