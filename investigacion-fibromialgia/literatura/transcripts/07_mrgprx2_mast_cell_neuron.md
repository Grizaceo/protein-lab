## 1. Resumen Ejecutivo

Este documento analiza la fisiología del receptor **MRGPRX2** (*Mas-related G-protein coupled receptor X2*) y su papel central en la desgranulación de mastocitos y basófilos independiente de IgE. Se desglosa la comunicación neuroinmune bidireccional entre los neuropéptidos sensory-neuronales (Substancia P, CGRP) y los mastocitos tisulares / basófilos en los ganglios de la raíz dorsal (DRG) y la dermis, reforzando el modelo donde los autoanticuerpos IgG actúan cooperativamente vía receptores FcγRIIIa y MRGPRX2 para inducir sensibilización periférica.

## 2. Transcripción Sintetizada y Cronológica del Simposio Neuroinmune

```text
[00:00 - 06:00] EL RECEPTOR NO CANÓNICO MRGPRX2 Y DESGRANULACIÓN INDEPENDIENTE DE IgE
"Historically, mast cell activation was attributed almost exclusively to IgE crosslinking of Fc-epsilon-RI receptors. 
However, the discovery of MRGPRX2 (Mas-related G-protein coupled receptor X2, human ortholog of mouse MrgprB2) fundamentally altered our understanding of neurogenic inflammation. 
MRGPRX2 is expressed selectively on connective tissue mast cells and basophils, acting as a promiscuous receptor for basic secretagogues, neuropeptides, and endogenous cationic peptides."

[06:01 - 13:45] EL SÍNAPSE NEURO-INMUNOLÓGICO: NEUROPÉPTIDOS Y MASTOCITOS
"In peripheral tissue and sensory ganglia, nociceptors and mast cells exist in intimate physical contact (<100 nm gap), forming a functional neuro-immune synapse. 
When sensory A-delta and C fibers undergo activation, they release neuropeptides including Substance P, CGRP, and VIP from their peripheral terminals. 
Substance P binds directly to MRGPRX2 on adjacent mast cells with high affinity, triggering instantaneous Gi/q-mediated intracellular calcium elevation and rapid degranulation without requiring IgE sensitization."

[13:46 - 22:15] COOPERACIÓN ENTRE Fc-gamma-RIIIa E INTERACCIÓN DE IgG AUTOREACTIVA
"In the context of autoimmune fibromyalgia (Goebel et al.), auto-IgG antibodies localized in the DRG bind to Fc-gamma-RIIIa (CD16) receptors on tissue-resident mast cells and circulating basophils. 
Simultaneously, activated nociceptors release Substance P into the DRG interstitium, engaging MRGPRX2. 
This dual activation—co-stimulation of Fc-gamma-RIIIa and MRGPRX2—exhibits synergistic potency, triggering explosive release of tryptase, histamine, TNF-alpha, and NGF. 
Tryptase cleaves Protease-Activated Receptor 2 (PAR2) on sensory neurons, inducing prolonged hyperalgesia."

[22:16 - 30:00] IMPLICACIONES FARMACOLÓGICAS Y PEQUEÑAS MOLÉCULAS ANTAGONISTAS DE MRGPRX2
"Targeting MRGPRX2 offers a precise way to disarm neurogenic mast cell degranulation without causing generalized immunosuppression. 
Small-molecule MRGPRX2 antagonists (e.g., QWF, triazole derivatives) effectively block Substance P- and IgG-mediated mast cell activation, reducing mechanical allodynia in animal models of neuropathic and autoimmune pain. 
Combining MRGPRX2 antagonists with Fc-receptor blockers represents a potential dual-action peripheral therapy for Fibromyalgia Subtype A."
```
