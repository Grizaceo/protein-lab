## A) Hipótesis Autoinmune

1. **Goebel et al. (2021)** — "Research Recommendations Following the Discovery of Pain Sensitising IgG Autoantibodies in Fibromyalgia Syndrome"
   - Journal: Pain Medicine
   - DOI: 10.1093/pm/pnab342 / PMC9157149
   - PDF: ✅ Goebel_2021_PainMed_ResearchRecommendations.pdf
   - Hallazgo: IgG de pacientes FM se une a DRG en ratones → induce dolor

   - Journal: Brain, Behavior, and Immunity
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.bbi.2023.08.012 / PMID: 37683961
   - PDF: ✅ Krock_2023_BrainBehavImmun_AntiSGC_IgG.pdf
   - Hallazgo: Anti-satellite glia IgG correlaciona con severidad de síntomas

## C) Estrés Oxidativo + Disfunción Mitocondrial

4. **Ho, Ryan, Holle (2025)** — "Redox reactions in chronic pain: mechanisms and relevance in fibromyalgia"
   - Journal: Frontiers in Pain Research
   - DOI: 10.3389/fpain.2025.1593908 / PMC12106312
   - PDF: ✅ Ho_2025_FrontiersPain_RedoxChronicPain.pdf
   - Hallazgo: Elevado MDA, 4-HNE; reducido CoQ10, SOD, catalasa; NRF2 comprometido

5. **Otlu & Kıran (2025)** — "Endoplasmic reticulum stress and oxidative imbalance is the missing link in fibromyalgia pathophysiology"
   - Journal: Scientific Reports (Nature)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-20737-9
   - PDF: ⚠️ No descargable directamente
   - Hallazgo: Estrés del RE + desbalance oxidativo → neuroinflamación → sensibilización central

6. **Ghasemikhah et al. (2025)** — "Dysregulation of lipid metabolism, energy production, and oxidative stress in ME/CFS, GWS, and FM"
   - Journal: Frontiers in Neuroscience
   - DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2025.1498981
   - PDF: ✅ Ghasemikhah_2025_FrontiersNeurosci_LipidMetabolism.pdf
   - Hallazgo: Estrés oxidativo sistémico contribuye a disfunción mitocondrial en FM

## E) Biomarcadores + ML

8. **Li et al. (2026)** — "EEG connectivity features associated with fibromyalgia revealed by machine learning"
   - Journal: Frontiers in Pain Research
   - DOI: 10.3389/fpain.2025.1704444 / PMID: 41625159 / PMC12852373
   - PDF: ⚠️ No descargable directamente
   - Hallazgo: 5 features en banda gamma → 99.57% accuracy (463 participantes)

9. **Zhao et al. (2025)** — "Identification of diagnostic biomarkers for fibromyalgia using gene expression analysis and machine learning"

   - PDF: ✅ Zhao_2025_FrontiersGenetics_DYRK3_RGS17_ARHGEF37.pdf

10. **Zeng & Liao (2025)** — "Machine learning for the diagnosis of fibromyalgia based on rs-fMRI and DTI"
